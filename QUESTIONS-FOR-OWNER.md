# Questions about the Legacy Collection

Everything needed to put the six products on the website properly.
Short answers are completely fine.

---

## 1. How does someone buy these?

Are LC1 to LC6 six separate finished products, where a customer picks one and
orders it as it is?

Or is each one a box whose contents can be changed, where a customer ticks and
unticks what goes inside before ordering?

## 2. What are they actually called?

What should each of the six be named on the website? Real names sell, codes
like "LC1" do not.

## 3. Do the prices include packaging?

The prices given are LC1 1299, LC2 2099, LC3 1699, LC4 2599, LC5 1499,
LC6 1299.

Is that the final amount the customer pays? Or is packaging charged on top, and
if so how much?

Right now the website adds a separate packaging charge to the total, so this
needs to be right or the total shown will be wrong.

## 4. Which photo belongs to which one?

Six photos were sent, named "Legacy collection 1" through "Legacy collection 6".
It has been assumed that the numbers match, so "Legacy collection 1" is LC1 and
its price is 1299.

Please confirm, because if the order is different, every price on the site will
sit under the wrong picture.

## 5. What is in each one?

One or two lines describing each item would be ideal. What it is, what it is
made of, roughly how big it is.

Size matters more than expected here: in the photos each piece is being held in
a palm, so people will want to know the actual measurements before buying.

## 6. What happens to the other four collections?

The website currently shows five collections, but only the Legacy Collection
has real products. The other four are filled with invented names and invented
prices from the original demo.

Three options:

- Remove them until they have real products
- Leave them showing as they are
- Show them named but marked "coming soon", so people can see the range without
  being able to order something that does not exist

## 7. Are bulk orders wanted?

Return gifts for weddings and functions usually sell in quantities of 50 to 200.

At the moment the website has no way for a customer to say how many they want,
so the biggest possible order cannot even be requested. Should there be a
quantity option, and is there a different price for larger quantities?

## 8. A few practical ones

- Which areas are delivered to, and roughly how long does delivery take?
- Is delivery charged, and if so how much?
- Should everything continue to go through WhatsApp, or should the website take
  payment directly at some point?
- Can items be personalised, for example a name or a handwritten note, and does
  that cost extra?
